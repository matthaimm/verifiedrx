Know the Difference

503A vs 503B — what it means for your practice.

Not all compounding pharmacies are the same. Understanding the distinction helps you source smarter, stay compliant, and serve your patients better.

503A — Patient-Specific

Traditional compounding pharmacies.

503A pharmacies compound medications for individual patients based on a valid prescription. These are patient-specific, meaning each compound is made to order for a named individual.

  • Requires a prescription for each patient
  • Compounds shipped directly to patient or clinic
  • Ideal for customized dosing and delivery methods
  • State-board regulated with USP standards
  • Best for: peptides, HRT, specialty protocols
  • In all 50 states
503B — Outsourcing Facilities

FDA-registered outsourcing facilities.

503B facilities can produce large batches of compounded drugs without patient-specific prescriptions. They are federally regulated by the FDA and subject to cGMP standards — the same rigor as commercial manufacturers.

  • No patient-specific prescription required
  • Bulk ordering available for clinic stock
  • FDA-registered and cGMP compliant
  • Higher consistency and scalability
  • Best for: office-use compounds, bulk, IV solutions
  • 6–12 month BUD's offered
